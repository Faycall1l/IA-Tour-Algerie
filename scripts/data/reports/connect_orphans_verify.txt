T2 verification (2026-08-11)
============================
Goal: 231 stations with no line_stops were unreachable in the DB-based
routing graph (TransitGraph.load builds edges only between consecutive
stops of a line).

Fix:
  - New `transfers` table (migration b3a9c1d2e4f5): undirected walking edges
    with distance_m + walking_time_min + source; unique index on the
    unordered station pair (idempotent re-runs).
  - TransitGraph.load now loads StationTransfer rows and adds bidirectional
    walking edges (mode=walking, operator=Walking, weight=walking_time_min);
    route builder emits a distinct "Marche à pied" segment for them.
  - scripts/data/connect_orphan_stations.py: grid spatial index (1 km cells,
    ring expansion with best-distance early exit) maps each orphan to its
    nearest *served* station (one on >=1 line); walking time at 80 m/min;
    edges capped at 5 km (longer walks are not useful transfers).

Result:
  - transfers inserted: 222 (avg 957 m, max 3,854 m; 0 edges > 5 km)
  - unmatched: 9 (honest gaps - nearest served station > 5 km):
    Gare Routiere de Illizi (214 km), Gare Routiere de In Salah (8.2 km),
    Akbou/Bejaia/El Kseur/Issers/Oued Ghir/Tazmalt train stations
    (5.3-16.5 km - these are real SNTF stops absent from DB line_stops;
    follow-up: add them to their SNTF line), Oued Ghir, unnamed bus stop.
  - Recoverable orphans: 222/231 (96%).

Tests: tests/services/test_transit_routing_walking.py (2 new) - orphan gets
a bidirectional walking edge; route from orphan includes walking segment.
17/17 transport API tests still pass.
